## Canonical interpretation

There is ONE FORENSIC FSM.

The database admission states are sequential predicates inside that FSM:

`DB_EXISTENCE -> DB_BINDING -> SECRET_RESOLUTION -> DB_TLS_ADMISSION -> NETWORK_ORIGIN_PROOF -> DB_ROUND_TRIP -> PROMOTION`

A PASS belongs only to the gate that generated it.

## Non-inheritance rule

`DB_EXISTENCE=PASS` does not mean `DB_BINDING=PASS`.

`DB_BINDING=PASS` does not mean `DB_TLS_ADMISSION=PASS`.

`DB_TLS_ADMISSION=PASS` does not mean `NETWORK_ORIGIN_PROOF=PASS`.

`NETWORK_ORIGIN_PROOF=PASS` does not mean `DB_ROUND_TRIP=PASS`.

Only a fresh, admissible `DB_ROUND_TRIP=PASS` may satisfy the final upstream predicate for promotion, and promotion remains its own decision gate.

## Forensic meaning

These are not two forensic states. They are multiple gate states within one forensic state machine. They describe different facts:

1. resource existence;
2. authorization/binding;
3. secret resolution;
4. transport security;
5. runtime/network provenance;
6. actual durable evidence integrity.

Each fact needs its own evidence.

Historical receipts are immutable history. A stale allocation/runtime receipt cannot be inherited by a new allocation. A previous PASS can be quarantined but not rewritten into a fresh PASS.
